## Limitations

- This review proves remediation owner fulfillment readiness before applying it to the production replacement submission; it does not collect authority evidence.
- A ready owner fulfillment review still requires submission regeneration, source snapshot collection, intake verification, manifest rebuild, readiness verification, and production replacement closure.
- Placeholder production-authority URIs intentionally keep the review blocked until owners supply live authority sources.
